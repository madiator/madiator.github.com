Recent Work & Impact

I started Bespoke Labs in 2024. We focus on RL environment curation and data curation.

Impact from Google DeepMind

  1. First to productionize TPUs for recommender systems at YouTube — reshaped YouTube's compute strategy.
  2. Demonstrated the gains of scaling recommender models — reshaped YouTube's modeling strategy.
  3. Introduced Semantic IDs and Generative Retrieval for Recommender Systems, now deployed or researched across YouTube, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Alibaba, Spotify, Meta, and Kuaishou. This has completely changed the course of research being done in the recommender systems community because it laid down the foundation for using generative AI for recommender models.
  4. Over the course of my tenure at Google, I had more than 50 peer and spot bonues, 5+ perfy awards, 1 best paper award, 1 feats of engineering award.

Impact from Bespoke Labs

  1. Instrumental in getting OpenThoughts started (origin thread).
  2. Core contributors to Terminal Bench, the most popular agentic benchmark.
